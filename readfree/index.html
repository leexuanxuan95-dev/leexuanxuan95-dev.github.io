Read Free

Ninety-eight public-domain classics in a reader with no account and no ads.

Read Free is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. Books you have already opened read offline; opening a new one needs a connection.

How it works

Pick a book from the shelves. The first time you open it, its text is downloaded once; after that it reads offline for good. Pages are laid out for your type size and face, a tap on either edge turns the page, and your place, bookmarks and settings stay on your iPhone.

Every title was first published in 1929 or earlier and its author died in 1955 or earlier, so each is in the public domain in the United States and in every life-plus-70 country. Where a book is a translation, the translation meets the same test. Each book's own screen states the dates it rests on.

Read Free is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Project Gutenberg and does not use its trademark; the distributor's header, footer and licence text are removed from every file before it is stored or shown, and the author — and translator, where there is one — are credited instead.

Read Free Full Access

The free version includes the 10 books on the Essentials shelf — whole books, not samples — with 3 bookmarks per book, the Paper theme and the Serif face.

Read Free Full Access opens all 98 books on every shelf, removes the bookmark limit, adds three more themes and three more typefaces, and keeps reading statistics on the device.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no per-book purchases. Cancelling never takes away a downloaded book, a bookmark or a reading position.

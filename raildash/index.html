Rail Dash

An endless three-rail runner whose track is generated as you run and proven passable before you see it.

Rail Dash is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. It works completely offline.

How it works

Swipe left or right to change rail, up to jump a barrier, down to roll under a sign. A parked train fills its rail — get out of it. Coins are worth five metres each. The track is generated from a seed as you run and every stretch is checked for a way through before it appears, so a run only ends when you miss something, never because the game dealt an impossible hand. After a crash you can run a fresh track or the very same one again.

There are no levels, no energy, no timers and no server: the whole game is on your iPhone and works offline.

Rail Dash Pro

The free version is the whole game: every track, every distance, one runner (Ember), coins banked at ×1 and no revives.

Rail Dash Pro unlocks the other four runners, one revive per run, and banks coins at ×2.

There is no advertising in this game whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no coin packs. Cancelling keeps your best distance and banked coins; the runner goes back to Ember.

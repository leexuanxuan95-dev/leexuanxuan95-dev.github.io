Support

Email jasperabundant@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

Is this a real narrator?

No. The narration is synthesised on your iPhone by the same system voices used for Spoken Content, so it sounds like a good text-to-speech voice, not like a recorded audiobook. That is what lets every book be free and offline. You can download higher-quality voices in the iPhone Settings app under Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices; with Pro you can pick any of them.

Are these books really free to listen to?

Yes. Every title was first published in 1929 or earlier and its author died in 1955 or earlier — public domain in the United States and in life-plus-70 countries. Translations meet the same test. Each book's own screen shows the dates it rests on, and the app checks those dates against every entry on every build.

Where does the text come from?

From a public digitising project's servers, downloaded once when you first open a book. Listen Free is not affiliated with that project and does not use its trademark; its header, footer and licence text are stripped before the book is stored, and the author is credited instead.

Do I lose my books if I cancel?

No. Downloaded books, listening positions and bookmarks stay on your device. Books outside Start Here simply stop being offered until you resubscribe, and Pro voices and speeds go back to the free ones.

The chapters look wrong for one book.

Chapters are found from the book's own headings, and a few old files have unusual layouts. The book still plays from start to finish; the chapter list is only for jumping around. Tell us the title and we will look at that file.

Why did a download fail?

Usually no connection, or the source being briefly unavailable. The app tells you which, saves nothing partial, and lets you try again. Books you already have are unaffected.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Listen Free, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Pro isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Mostly. Every book you have opened before is on your iPhone and plays without a connection, because the narration is generated on the device — that is the point of downloading the text once. Opening a book for the first time needs a connection to fetch its text. If you are offline, the app says so and nothing partial is saved.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

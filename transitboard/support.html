Are these live arrival times?

No. Transit Board shows the agency's scheduled departures from its published timetable. It does not know about delays, detours or cancellations, and it never will, because it has no server and no live data. That is the trade-off that lets it work with no signal.

Where does the timetable come from?

From the transit agency itself. Agencies publish their schedules in an open format called GTFS; Transit Board downloads that file once, from the agency's own address, and reads it on your iPhone. The list shows the date each address was last checked. Transit Board is not affiliated with any agency.

My city's timetable says it has expired.

Agencies publish schedules that cover a few weeks or months. When yours runs out, tap Get on the city again to download the current one. Your saved stops stay.

My city isn't in the list.

The list holds agencies whose published feed we could download and read on the day shown, and that are small enough to import on a phone. Tell us the city; if its agency publishes a usable feed, it can be added in an update.

Does it use my location?

Only when you tap “Stops near me”, and only on your iPhone: the coordinate is compared with the stops in the timetable already on the device, then discarded. It is never stored or sent. You can search stops by name instead and never grant location access.

Do I lose my cities if I cancel?

No. Downloaded cities stay on your device. Saved stops beyond the free limit of three are removed, and the app tells you how many.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Transit Board, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Pro isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Yes — that is the point. Every city you have added is on your iPhone and works with no signal at all. Adding a city needs a connection to fetch its timetable file from the agency (a few megabytes). If you are offline, the app says so and nothing partial is saved.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

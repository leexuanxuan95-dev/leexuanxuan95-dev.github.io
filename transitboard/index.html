Transit Board

Bus, subway and train timetables that work with no signal — the agency's own schedule, on your iPhone.

Transit Board is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. Cities you have added work offline; adding a new one needs a connection.

How it works

Add a city on the Cities tab. Its whole timetable — every stop, every route, every scheduled departure — is downloaded once from the transit agency's published schedule (the open GTFS format every major agency provides) and stored on your iPhone. From then on the city works underground, on a plane, or with no data plan: search a stop by name or tap “Stops near me”, and the board shows the next scheduled departures in the agency's own time zone.

Save the stops you use and the Saved tab shows their next departures at a glance. With Pro, pick a departure and get a notification a few minutes before it leaves.

The times are the agency's scheduled times, not live vehicle positions: Transit Board does not know about delays, detours or cancellations. Agencies replace their schedules a few times a year; when a city's timetable expires the app says so and you download it again. Each city in the list was last checked on the date shown beside it. Transit Board is not affiliated with any transit agency.

Transit Board Pro

The free version includes 1 city timetable — the whole city, not a sample — with 3 saved stops, stop search, stops near you and the departure board.

Transit Board Pro removes the city limit (add as many as you travel to), removes the saved-stop limit, and adds departure reminders.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no per-city purchases. Cancelling never removes a downloaded city; saved stops beyond the free limit are trimmed, and the app tells you.
